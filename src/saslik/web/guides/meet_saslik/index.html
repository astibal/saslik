01

The big picture

Meet Saslik.

A workspace for understanding traffic.

Saslik brings our in-house tools together. You use them through its web portal.

  • Smithproxy inspects traffic passing through the workspace.
  • Spider explores live sessions and lets you script and modify traffic.
  • PCAP Viewer opens saved capture files.
More details · about what you see

  • Our tools, built to work together

    Smithproxy, Spider, PCAP Viewer, Skewer and Tuntom are developed within our own project ecosystem. Saslik brings the analysis tools together in a workspace. Skewer is their client-side companion: it helps connect your test applications and prepare certificate trust. Tuntom provides the tunnel transport for remote test traffic. The underlying operating system and libraries still include third-party software.

  • Observe — then change the experiment

    Spider is more than a live session list. Traffic scripting lets you change live traffic and investigate how an application responds. Use a controlled test setup when modifying exchanges.

  • Keep experiments separate

    A workspace groups its configuration and capture data. You can keep different saved setups and run one workspace at a time.

02

Your starting point

The portal is your front door.

One address. Two purposes.

Come here to prepare your device — and return here to work with the results.

  • Connect It: download Skewer and find connection details.
  • Sign in: open Spider and PCAP Viewer.
  • Administration: manage the workspace and its components.
More details · about what you see

  • Connect It is the public starting point

    You can reach the download and connection instructions without a portal account. Installing a client and gaining access to analysis or administration are separate tasks.

  • Pairing and signing in are different

    Pairing authorizes a client connection. Portal login opens the analysis tools and administration; ask your administrator for those credentials.

  • The portal need not be on your device

    Saslik may run on a VM or a remote machine. The browser is your window into that workspace, including its saved capture storage.

03

On your device

Skewer gets your apps ready for inspection.

The recommended companion.

Install Skewer on the device whose traffic you want to inspect.

  • It helps install CA trust for HTTPS inspection.
  • Attach to Skewer directs your chosen app’s traffic into Saslik.
  • It handles routing, using a direct route or tunnel as appropriate.
More details · about what you see

  • You can choose the route

    Auto chooses a directly reachable gateway when available, otherwise Tuntom. Direct opts out of tunneling; Enforced tunnel uses a tunnel even when a direct route is possible. Disable routing before switching modes.

  • The tunnel has its own component

    Tuntom is our in-house tunnel transport. On Linux, the Skewer client uses a separately installed Tuntom package. On the other supported client platforms, the transport is built into Skewer rather than installed separately. Saslik provides the server side.

  • Enforced really means enforced

    A failed tunnel does not silently turn into a direct connection. This preserves the chosen routing policy while the connection recovers.

  • Manual setup remains possible

    Skewer is a helper, not a requirement. You can configure proxies, routing and CA trust yourself — and in some environments, manual setup may be the only workable option. Skewer makes the common cases easier without taking that choice away.

More details · about what you see

  • Live scripting is an active tool

    Spider lets you inspect sessions and script changes to live traffic. A saved capture is a record to examine later, not a way to change an exchange that has already happened.

  • Saved captures are not an automatic archive

    Recording must be enabled and produce files. A session visible in Spider does not necessarily have a corresponding saved capture.

  • The data stays with the workspace

    PCAP Viewer browses the workspace’s capture storage, including when Saslik is remote. Closing your browser is not the same as deleting those files.

  • Use test data deliberately

    Inspected traffic and captures can contain messages, tokens or credentials. Keep experiments scoped and review captures before sharing them.
